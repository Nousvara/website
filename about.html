About the studio

A maker-led studio, built on two decades of engineering

Nousvara is one experienced engineer building focused software, with specialists brought in when a specific tool needs them. The years spent shipping systems at scale are the reason the tools are private, light and honest — that experience is why you can trust what's built here, not a service being sold to you.

The background

From hardware to cloud, at real scale

Two decades across the full chain — software for life-critical devices, telematics and connected platforms, and the large-scale data and cloud systems behind them. That range is why the tools here span different problems yet share one instinct: keep the user's data theirs, and hold up under real use.

How the studio works

One maker, honest scope

Products first

Nousvara is a product studio. The tools are the work. Each one starts as a real problem, gets built small and honest, and is used before it's sold.

Specialists when needed

For a tool that needs a specific skill — a domain, a design, a legal review — the right specialist is brought in for that piece, with their role made clear.

Occasionally, senior technical advisory work comes up — helping a team think through a hard technology decision. That's offered selectively and by conversation, not as a menu of services on this site. If you have a decision like that, reach out and we'll see if it's a fit.
