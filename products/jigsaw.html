What it does

Simple to pick up, yours to keep

  • Use your own photo — from the camera or your gallery.
  • Choose a grid size from 2×2 up to 6×6.
  • Tap a piece, then another, to swap them into place.
  • Move counter and timer track your solve.
  • Five colour themes, and sound effects you can switch off.
  • Your photo never leaves the phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
