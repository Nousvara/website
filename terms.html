Changing devices

Contact us when your device code changes

A hardware replacement, operating-system reinstallation or similar change can produce a new device code. Email contact@nousvara.com with the purchase email, old license details and new device code. Legitimate replacement requests are handled manually. Because Swaro has no remote license server, an old key cannot be remotely revoked.
