About the studio

Small software, built with large-scale engineering discipline

Nousvara is an independent software studio based in Gurugram, India. Our founder's work spans automotive, EV charging, medical devices, agriculture and industrial IoT, from writing firmware in hardware labs to leading teams that run cloud data platforms in production. Nousvara applies that experience to focused products that people can own outright.

The background

Where our experience comes from

Twenty years across the whole chain of a connected product: the device, the firmware on it, the network in between, and the cloud platforms that turn its data into decisions.

Embedded and safety-critical systems

Firmware in C and real-time operating systems for medical and automotive devices, and vehicle protocols such as CAN, J1939 and OBD-II.

Connected products and IoT

Device-to-cloud communication, over-the-air updates, MQTT and Bluetooth, and fleet and EV charging platforms built on OCPP and OCPI.

Cloud and data platforms

Cloud-native systems on AWS and Azure, real-time pipelines with Kafka, Spark and Databricks, time-series databases, monitoring, security and disaster recovery.

Engineering leadership

Building cross-functional teams across embedded, backend, mobile and analytics, aligning roadmaps with business goals, and keeping cloud costs under control.

How the studio works

How we build

Your data stays under your control

Wherever a product runs, on your device, in the cloud or on hardware, data moves only when the product needs it to. You can always see what's collected, export it and delete it.

Engineered for real conditions

We build for modest hardware, weak networks and long service lives, not ideal lab setups, and test for what happens when things go wrong, not only when they go right.

Clear pricing, no lock-in

You see the full price before you buy, get a fair way to try or return a product, and are never trapped by subscriptions you can't leave or formats you can't export.

Transparent by default

Every release is documented, downloads can be verified, and we open-source our work where that serves the people using it.
