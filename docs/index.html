What drives us

The principles behind every tool

Your data stays yours

We do not own or sell your data, and we do not run on advertising. The core of most tools needs no account at all.

Useful without heavy requirements

Good software should work on the laptop you already have, without demanding a constant connection.

Solves one real problem

Each tool starts from a problem someone actually has, often one we hit ourselves, and stays focused on it.

Honest by default

Clear pricing, clear limits, no dark patterns, and no claims before a product is ready for people to use.

Who's behind it

The engineering behind Nousvara

Nousvara was founded by an engineer with twenty years of building connected products end to end: firmware for safety-critical medical and automotive devices, IoT and edge-to-cloud systems, and cloud data platforms that now serve over half a million connected vehicles.

We bring the same discipline to small tools. They're reliable, light on hardware, and they keep your data on your device unless there's a real reason to move it.
