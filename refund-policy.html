Refund policy

14-day money-back guarantee

Full refund within 14 days of purchase, for any reason. Try a paid license with no risk — if it's not right for you, email us and we'll refund it.

The policy

Simple and honest

  1. 1

    Full refund within 14 days

    If you bought a license and want a refund, you have 14 days from the date of purchase. No questions asked, for any reason.

  2. 2

    How to request one

    Email us at contact@nousvara.com from the address you used to buy, and mention that you'd like a refund. That's all we need to start it.

  3. 3

    How you get your money back

    Refunds are issued through our payment provider, back to your original payment method. It usually takes a few business days for your bank or card provider to show it, depending on them.

Try before you buy. Swaro comes with a free 14-day trial that includes every feature, with no card required. If you're unsure, start the trial first — the refund guarantee is there for the times a purchase still doesn't work out.

Good to know

A few details

  • The 14 days are counted from the date of purchase, not the date you activate your license.
  • The refund is for the license fee you paid. Swaro runs fully offline, so there are no usage charges or extras to reconcile.
  • After a refund, the license for that purchase is no longer active. You're always welcome to buy again later.
  • The guarantee covers one refund per customer. Because a complete free trial lets you evaluate every feature before paying, we may decline a refund where there is clear evidence of misuse, such as repeated buy-and-refund activity.
  • This policy is in addition to any rights you may have under the consumer law that applies where you live.
